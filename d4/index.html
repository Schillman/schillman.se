The season

What Season 14 actually asks of you

Season 14 tears the wall between Sanctuary and Pandemonium, and almost everything worth having comes out of one chain: break a Death's Head Idol, hold the Rupture open, finish the right kind of Rupture for a Realmwalker, take the Deathtoll Chamber, bank Superior Lair Keys, and spend them two at a time on the Corrupted Reaper.

The four guides below are that chain taken apart, plus the two systems most likely to be sitting unused in your game while you run it.

  • Season of Death Awakening, explained

    The whole chain in one page. Pandemonium Ruptures and their three sizes, Death's Head Idols and Tears, when a Realmwalker can spawn and when it cannot, Deathtoll Chambers, Superior Lair Keys, the Corrupted Reaper, and how Glints of Hope and Pandemonium Fragments turn into Mythic Uniques.

  • War Plans: the board, the trees, and what to rush

    Where the board is, the seven separate activity trees and why spreading across them is a trap, how Activity Experience and skill points are earned, rerolling with New Plans, party sync and what it costs the initiator, and the nine confirmed nodes worth the first points.

  • Helltides and the cinder economy

    Aberrant Cinder drop rates, the expiry rule that quietly wastes most players' cinders, what every Tortured Gift costs, the Threat meter, Kixxarth, and why an hour in Helltide is worth more this season.

  • Escalations and the Horadric Reserve affixes

    The three dungeon chain into Astaroth, how positive and negative affixes stack as you climb, and a full table of every Reserve affix with what its eight chests actually contain, so you can pick the run that pays the material you are short of.

All of them, with the order to read them in, are indexed on the guides page.

The tool

The Season 14 Farming Ledger

Season guides tell you what is good. They rarely tell you what you have already done, which by week three is the thing you actually need. The Ledger is the second half of that: a checklist of the 48 farming decisions that matter in Season 14, grouped into 13 categories and tickable as you go.

Each entry is one concrete action with a short note on why it is worth your time, when in the season to do it, and what it competes with. Items are tagged early game, late game, high priority, new this season or nerfed, so you can filter the list down to just the part of the season you are in right now.

Design

How it works, and why it is built this way

  • Progress lives in your browser, not on a server Ticked items are written to localStorage the moment you click. There is no account and nothing is uploaded, which also means progress is per browser and per device. Clearing site data wipes it.
  • Export and Import instead of sync One button copies your progress to the clipboard as a small text blob, another pastes it back in somewhere else. That is the whole sync story, and it needs no infrastructure to keep running years from now.
  • One file, no build step The entire ledger is a single HTML file with its data, styles and logic inline. You can save it to disk and it keeps working with the network off, which is the point when you are alt-tabbing out of a game.
  • Unverified details are labelled as unverified Season content gets patched and terminology drifts. Where a detail could not be confirmed against current patch notes or War Plans data, the item says so rather than guessing, so you can check your own board.
