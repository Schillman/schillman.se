Start here

If you are new to the season

Read the Season of Death Awakening page first. Season 14 hangs almost everything on one activity chain, and the other guides all assume you know how that chain fits together. After that, the order below is roughly the order the season asks the questions in: what to do with the board, where to farm, what to spend, what the endgame item actually costs.

  • Season of Death Awakening, explained

    The whole chain in one page. Pandemonium Ruptures and their three sizes, Death's Head Idols and Tears, when a Realmwalker can spawn and when it cannot, Deathtoll Chambers, Superior Lair Keys, the Corrupted Reaper, and how Glints of Hope and Pandemonium Fragments turn into Mythic Uniques.

  • War Plans: the board, the trees, and what to rush

    The system most likely to be sitting unused in your game. Where the board is, the seven separate activity trees and why spreading across them is a trap, how Activity Experience and skill points are earned, rerolling with New Plans, party sync and what it costs the initiator, and the nine confirmed nodes worth the first points.

  • Helltides and the cinder economy

    Aberrant Cinder drop rates, the expiry rule that quietly wastes most players' cinders, what every Tortured Gift costs including Hell's Prize, the Threat meter as a reward mechanism rather than a punishment, Kixxarth, and why an hour in Helltide is worth more in Season 14 than in any season before it.

  • Escalations and the Horadric Reserve affixes

    The three dungeon chain into Astaroth, how positive and negative affixes stack as you climb, and a full table of every Reserve affix with what its eight chests actually contain, so you can pick the run that pays the material you are short of.

  • Mythic Uniques: every route, and what each one costs

    Mythic Uniques 3.0, where Mythic became a quality applied to any Unique rather than a rarity of its own. The four acquisition routes side by side with exact costs, the Crafted tag rule that limits you to one equipped, where Pandemonium Fragments actually come from, and what patch 3.1.1 changed.

  • Levelling and Paragon: the Pit is not the answer any more

    Why the Pit fell from best experience in the game to the worst, the Helltide Hellwyrm method step by step including the Threat level 2 requirement and what raises it, what a wyrm is actually worth with Writhe and Rot taken, and the savagely back loaded Paragon curve that puts you at 13% of the way to 300 when you hit Paragon 240.

  • The Undercity: Tributes, Attunement and runes

    The only activity where you choose the reward before you start. The three floor run and its timer, why a better Tribute gives you less time, the exact seconds each enemy type buys back, Attunement and what it gates, every Tribute and what it targets, Bargains, and why Greater Harmony is the only reliable route to Legendary Runes.

Scope

What is not here

What you will not find here is build guides, tier lists or leaderboard theorycrafting. I am an ordinary seasonal player, not a competitive pusher, and Maxroll and Icy Veins will serve you far better for that. These pages cover mechanics and farming decisions, which is the part I actually have something to add on.

The tool
