Start here

If you are new to the season

Read the Season of Death Awakening page first. Season 14 hangs almost everything on one activity chain, and the other guides all assume you know how that chain fits together. After that, the order below is roughly the order the season asks the questions in: what to do with the board, where to farm, what to spend, what the endgame item actually costs.

  • Season of Death Awakening, explained The whole chain in one page. Pandemonium Ruptures and their three sizes, Death's Head Idols and Tears, when a Realmwalker can spawn and when it cannot, Deathtoll Chambers, Superior Lair Keys, the Corrupted Reaper, and how Glints of Hope and Pandemonium Fragments turn into Mythic Uniques.
  • War Plans: the board, the trees, and what to rush The system most likely to be sitting unused in your game. Where the board is, the seven separate activity trees and why spreading across them is a trap, how Activity Experience and skill points are earned, rerolling with New Plans, party sync and what it costs the initiator, and the nine confirmed nodes worth the first points.
  • Escalations and the Horadric Reserve affixes The three dungeon chain into Astaroth, how positive and negative affixes stack as you climb, and a full table of every Reserve affix with what its eight chests actually contain, so you can pick the run that pays the material you are short of.

How to read them

Dates, sources and what is not here

Every guide carries the date it was last checked against sources, directly under the title. Season content is patched constantly, so a page with an old date on it is a page to be suspicious of, and if your game disagrees with something here, your game is right. Patch 3.1.1 on 14 July 2026 in particular moved several numbers that older Season 14 guides across the whole community still quote.

Where sources disagree or a figure could not be confirmed, the page says so and tells you to read the value off your own game rather than quietly picking whichever number looked most plausible. There are a few of those, and they are marked.

What you will not find here is build guides, tier lists or leaderboard theorycrafting. I am an ordinary seasonal player who is careful about sourcing, not a competitive pusher, and Maxroll and Icy Veins will serve you far better for that. These pages cover mechanics and farming decisions, which is the part I actually have something to add on.
