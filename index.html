Current project

Season 14 Farming Ledger

Season guides tell you what is good. They rarely tell you what you have already done. The Ledger is the second half of that: a checklist of the 48 farming decisions that actually matter in Season 14, Season of Death Awakening, grouped into 13 categories and tickable as you go.

Each entry is one concrete action with a short note on why it is worth your time, when in the season to do it, and what it competes with. Items are tagged early game, late game, high priority, new this season or nerfed, so you can filter the list down to just the part of the season you are in right now.

Design

How it works, and why it is built this way

  • Progress lives in your browser, not on a server Ticked items are written to localStorage the moment you click. There is no account and nothing is uploaded, which also means progress is per browser and per device. Clearing site data wipes it.
  • Export and Import instead of sync One button copies your progress to the clipboard as a small text blob, another pastes it back in somewhere else. That is the whole sync story, and it needs no infrastructure to keep running years from now.
  • One file, no build step The entire ledger is a single HTML file with its data, styles and logic inline. You can save it to disk and it keeps working with the network off, which is the point when you are alt-tabbing out of a game.
  • Unverified details are labelled as unverified Season content gets patched and terminology drifts. Where a detail could not be confirmed against current patch notes or War Plans data, the item says so rather than guessing, so you can check your own board.
