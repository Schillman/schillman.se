Fastest route

Open an issue on GitHub

Every page and tool on this site is public source, so the quickest way to reach me about anything on it is an issue on the relevant repository. It lands in the same place I am already working, it keeps the thread attached to the thing being discussed, and other people can see the answer.

  • Something wrong in the Season 14 Farming Ledger, the checklist at d4.schillman.se: github.com/Schillman/farming-ledger/issues
  • Something wrong in a written guide or anywhere else on schillman.se: github.com/Schillman/schillman.se/issues
  • Not sure which, or you would rather not pick: github.com/Schillman

A GitHub account is free and takes a minute. If you would genuinely rather not create one, say so in whatever way you can reach me and I will work with it.

Corrections

What makes a correction easy to act on

Corrections are the single most useful thing anyone sends me, and I would much rather be told than left publishing something wrong. Season content gets patched constantly and a page written in week one can be stale by week four.

You do not need to format anything or be polite about it. But a correction I can verify gets fixed the same day, and one I cannot sits in a queue while I try to reproduce it. What helps:

  • The page it is on, and roughly which section or table row.
  • What it currently says, and what it should say instead.
  • Where you saw the correct version, a patch note, a guide site, or your own game. Your own game is a completely acceptable source, just say that is what it is.
  • If it changed in a patch, the patch number if you know it.

Everything published here carries the date it was last checked against sources, so if you are looking at a page with an old date on it, treat a disagreement with your game as the page being stale rather than you being wrong.

Bugs

If a tool is broken rather than wrong

The ledger stores your progress in your own browser, so the failure modes are usually local. Before reporting, it is worth knowing that progress is per browser and per device by design, and that clearing site data for the page wipes it. That is not a bug, and the Export button exists to move progress between devices.

For anything else, the browser and its version, the device, and what you clicked before it went wrong are enough for me to start. Screenshots help. If you lost progress, say so first, because that is the one thing I care about more than reproducing it neatly.

Other

Rights, advertising and everything else

  • Rights holders and takedown requests This is an unofficial fan site with no affiliation to Blizzard Entertainment. If you represent a rights holder and something here needs to change or come down, open an issue or reach me through GitHub above and I will act on it directly. I would rather fix it than argue about it.
  • Advertising and privacy questions What third party advertising stores in your browser, and how to opt out of personalised ads, is set out on the privacy and cookies page. If something there is unclear or you want data related to your visit dealt with, contact me through GitHub and say that is what it is about.
  • Sponsorship, guest posts and link requests The honest answer is no. This site carries no affiliate links, no sponsored placements and no paid rankings, and I am not looking to add any. Guest posts and link exchange requests are declined without exception, so please do not spend your time on the pitch.
  • Build help and theorycrafting I am not the right person. I am a fairly ordinary seasonal player who is careful about sourcing, not a leaderboard pusher, and the build communities on Maxroll, Icy Veins and the game's own subreddit will serve you far better than I will.
