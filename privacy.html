Summary

The short version

These are static pages. There is no account, no login, no contact form and no database. I do not collect, store or sell your personal information, and I run no analytics. The only third parties involved are Google, for the web fonts every page uses and for the advertising shown across the site, and Netlify, which hosts the files.

Local storage

What is stored on your device

The Season 14 Farming Ledger saves which items you have ticked off to your browser's localStorage, under a single key. That data never leaves your browser: it is not sent to me or to anyone else, and I cannot read it. It stays until you clear your browser's site data for the page, which erases it permanently.

Nothing else is written to your device by me. No cookies are set by this site itself.

Third parties

Advertising

This site displays advertising served by Google AdSense, on schillman.se and on its subdomains including d4.schillman.se. Google and its partners act independently here, and their technology, not mine, decides which ads you see. I do not choose the individual ads and advertising has no influence on what is written.

  • Google uses cookies and similar identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Third party vendors and ad networks may also serve ads on the site and may use their own cookies.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can turn off personalised advertising at Google My Ad Center, and opt out of third party vendors' use of cookies for personalised ads at aboutads.info. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent notice will ask for your choices on personalised ads before they are served, and you can change that choice at any time from the same notice.

Infrastructure

Web fonts and hosting

Every page loads the Cinzel and Barlow typefaces from Google Fonts. Requesting a font from Google's servers reveals your IP address and browser details to Google, as any request to any server does. Nothing else is passed along.

The files themselves are served by Netlify, which processes standard server request data such as your IP address in order to deliver them and to protect the site from abuse. This is described in the Netlify Privacy Policy.

Children

Age

These pages are not directed at children under 13, and I knowingly collect no information from anyone, of any age.

Contact

Changes and corrections

If any of the above changes, for example if analytics or a different ad provider is ever added, this page gets updated and the date at the top changes with it. Questions or corrections, including anything about advertising or data related to your visit: the contact page lists the ways to reach me.
